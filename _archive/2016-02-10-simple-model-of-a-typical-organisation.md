This is very much an initial draft, and is in response to the fact that I couldn't find a standard model out there [if someone has found one please share it!]. [The closest I've been pointed to so far is this from Bizzdesign](http://blog.bizzdesign.com/driving-business-outcomes-with-enterprise-architecture-as-a-knowledge-hub). But that is a bit too Enterprise Architecture Centric for my liking.
