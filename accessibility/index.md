*Last reviewed: 7 October 2026 (draft).*

We want everyone to be able to use this website, and aim to meet [WCAG 2.2 level AA](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/). We have not yet had a formal audit.

## What we have done

- Semantic HTML with a skip link, headings in order and labelled navigation.
- Clear keyboard focus styles, and everything works with a keyboard alone.
- Text and button colours checked against the AA contrast ratio (4.5:1 for normal text).
- Layouts that reflow on small screens and when text is enlarged, with no horizontal scrolling at phone width.
- Videos load only when you press a labelled play button. Animations are removed if your device asks for reduced motion.

## Known issues

- **Imported articles.** Older newsletter articles were moved over from LinkedIn. Some images have no description, and some formatting is imperfect. Embedded LinkedIn videos and posts appear as links to the original.
- **Videos.** Captions and transcripts depend on what is available on YouTube.
- **Diagrams.** Diagram descriptions are short summaries, not full text equivalents.
- **Third-party content.** We cannot control the accessibility of LinkedIn, YouTube, Spotify or Apple Podcasts.

## Feedback

If you find a barrier, or need something in a different format, please tell us via the [contact page]({{ '/contact/' | relative_url }}) and we will try to fix it. A dedicated email address and response time are TBC.
