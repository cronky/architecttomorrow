[A few months ago](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/cronky_architecttomorrow-activity-7252075773988106241-pYzK?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAAABj8NgBb3hswVC52Le1VqCxsFdyPrhsJvY), we asked for your input on a new slogan for Architect Tomorrow. Apologies for the delay in following up! After thoughtful community input and careful consideration, we've narrowed down to these three finalists. Each captures different aspects of our mission at the intersection of sustainability, business, technology, strategy and architecture.

### Vote for your favourite in the comments:

1️⃣ **"Strategy & Architecture for a Better Future"** • Directly connects our core focus on both strategy and architecture • Emphasises the positive impact we're working to create • Thanks to [Lisa Woodall](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ACoAAADlMnoBD9oAp32mOYDvikagJWnf8mFppYk) for this suggestion!

2️⃣ **"Designing Tomorrow's Enterprises Today"** • Balances immediate action with future vision • Emphasises the practical, outcome-focused nature of our work • Builds on a popular suggestion from our original brainstorming session

3️⃣ **"Navigating Sustainable Futures Together"** • Highlights our emphasis on sustainability across multiple dimensions • Reinforces the collaborative, community aspect of our work • Inspired by [Jonathan Huw Howells](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ACoAAAAQhTIBlHZQBOpvqZOJlmrVZUPMi76WMYo) thoughtful suggestion

Feel free to comment your choice (1, 2, or 3) and feel free to share why you like it.

The Architect Tomorrow community brings together Chief, Enterprise, Business, Security and IT Architects and related roles to collaborate and learn from each other. As we continue to grow and evolve (as per the last post the community is 5 years old now and things have moved on), we want a slogan that better reflects the mission and vision.

Voting closes once I feel it's had enough feedback (at least a week after posting this).

Cheers!

Ollie
