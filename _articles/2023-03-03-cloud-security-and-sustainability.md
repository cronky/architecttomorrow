Oops it is March and the newsletter for Feb didn't get out of drafts! Well better late than never here it is! And it features 2 great episodes - both relating to cloud architecture.

### Confidential and Trusted Computing with Christophe Parisel:

[Embedded LinkedIn content: view it on the original article](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/cloud-security-sustainability-oliver-cronk)

We start with a state of the nation on Cloud Security in 2023 and then move into different layers of Trusted Computing and then explore confidential computing and the impact it has on the shared responsibility model.

Christophe from Societe Generale shares fantastic thinking on Cloud Security in particular in highly regulated environments and Cloud vulnerabilities on his LinkedIn profile. We were joined on this one by Peter Chamberlin who is leading on Secure Engineering at Scott Logic.

### Is Serverless more Sustainable? With Daniel Bass:

[Embedded LinkedIn content: view it on the original article](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/cloud-security-sustainability-oliver-cronk)

Daniel Bass CTO and Co-Founder of Zeti (a clean vehicle financing start up) about effective Serverless Computing. Daniel gives a quick background of his career in Financial Services and then at Zeti.

We talked about effective Serverless Architectural thinking and the architecture transformation needed to really take advantage of cloud native.
