## The short version

This website does not use cookies, advertising or analytics, and does not ask you to create an account or hand over personal details.

## What the site loads

- **Hosting.** The site is served by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver pages and keep the service secure. See [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/). Whether Cloudflare Web Analytics is switched on is TBC.
- **YouTube.** Video embeds load only after you press play, and use the privacy-enhanced `youtube-nocookie.com` domain. Episode thumbnails are copied onto this site, so YouTube (Google) only sees your IP address if you choose to play a video.
- **LinkedIn.** Some older article images are still loaded from LinkedIn's servers. These are due to be copied onto this site (TBC).
- **Links out.** Links to LinkedIn, YouTube, Spotify, Apple Podcasts and other sites are governed by those sites' own policies.
- **Fonts and scripts.** The site uses your device's own fonts and a very small script of its own. It does not use third-party scripts.

## Newsletter and social media

Subscribing to the newsletter, following or commenting happens on LinkedIn, under [LinkedIn's privacy policy](https://www.linkedin.com/legal/privacy-policy). We do not hold a mailing list of our own at the moment (TBC).

## Your rights and contact

If you have questions or want something removed (for example a quote or photo of you), please use the [contact page]({{ '/contact/' | relative_url }}). Who is formally responsible for the data (the controller) is TBC.
