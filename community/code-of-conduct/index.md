Architect Tomorrow is a volunteer-powered community built on honest, multidisciplinary conversation. These are the standards we hold ourselves to, taken from our [participant guide]({{ '/community/get-involved/' | relative_url }}).

## What we ask of everyone

- **Keep it accessible.** Explain terminology and keep jargon in check.
- **Respect others' opinions.** Diversity of thought and background is what Architect Tomorrow is built on.
- **Share the floor.** Don't dominate conversations. Use your ears as much as your mouth.
- **Be constructive.** Focus on making things better rather than moaning.
- **Welcome everyone.** We are committed to making under-represented groups in tech visible, and expect everyone to be treated with dignity.
- **Stay independent.** Contributors are welcome from any organisation, but please do not use the community for sales pitches. *(Proposed.)*

## Reporting a concern

Tell [Oliver](https://www.linkedin.com/in/cronky/) or any regional lead on [the community page]({{ '/community/' | relative_url }}). A formal, confidential reporting route and the steps we would take are TBC.
