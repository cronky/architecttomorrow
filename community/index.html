Community

Architect Tomorrow is independent and community-led, with volunteer leads across many regions and themes worldwide. Everyone who appears is a volunteer, and nobody pays to play.

About Architect Tomorrow · Get involved · Code of conduct

Community leads

Regional leads look after Europe, the US, Australia and Africa. Selena and Darryl are regular co-hosts, although they don't appear in every episode.

Want to be part of it?

We are always interested in new voices and new ideas, whether that is a guest spot, a topic you want to explore or a region or theme you would like to help lead. Read the guide for participants and speakers, then message Oliver directly or post with #ArchitectTomorrow on LinkedIn.
